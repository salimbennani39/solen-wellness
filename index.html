Good to know.

Got another question? Our team is always happy to help. Message us, call us or ask the front desk.

Ask on WhatsApp
I've never done Pilates. Is that OK?

Yes. With five people per class, your instructor sees you the whole time and corrects your form as you go. Start with a welcome session and we'll take it from there.

What should I bring?

Grip socks are required on the reformer for your safety, stability and comfort. Forgot yours? Beautiful pairs are available at the studio.

Can I go straight to work after class?

That's the idea. The Refresh Room has a private shower, secure lockers and vanity essentials, so you leave ready for a meeting, a date or dinner at home.

What if I'm running late?

Classes start right on time to protect everyone's session. Life happens, so if you arrive a few minutes late, slip in softly and ease onto your reformer without breaking the flow.

How do cancellations work?

Cancel or reschedule at least 12 hours before class. Cancellations inside the 12-hour window count as a used class.

How do I book?

Send us a message on WhatsApp with the class you'd like and we'll find your spot. Packs work across Reformer, Mat Pilates and Yoga.
